## 🛡️ The Exam Validation Protocol

Before any exam voucher is purchased:
1. **The Ledger Check:** Bank account or Wise/Stripe wallet must show client payments or bounty payouts totaling at least $1.5 \times$ the exam cost.
2. **The Simulation Test:** Score $\ge 85\%$ on full-length timed diagnostic mocks (e.g. TutorialDojo for AWS, Killer.sh for CKA/CKS, Boson for CCNA).
3. **The Proof-of-Work Freeze:** The accompanying open-source capstone repository must be tagged, documentation verified, and live endpoints checked for 99.9% uptime.
