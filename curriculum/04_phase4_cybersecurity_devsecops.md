## 🧠 Part 1: The Production Teaching Plan

### Weeks 40–44: The Open Group TOGAF® 10 Framework
- **The Architecture Development Method (ADM):**
  - *Preliminary Phase:* Establishing architecture capability, principles, governance framework, and organizational context.
  - *Phase A (Architecture Vision):* Defining business goals, stakeholder management, and scope.
  - *Phase B (Business Architecture):* Business capability modeling, value streams, and organizational process mapping.
  - *Phase C (Information Systems Architecture):* Data architecture (entities, flow, governance) and application portfolio architecture.
  - *Phase D (Technology Architecture):* Cloud infrastructure, networking, platforms, and security standards.
  - *Phases E & F (Opportunities & Migration Planning):* Gap analysis, cost-benefit analysis (CapEx vs OpEx), roadmap scheduling.
  - *Phases G & H (Implementation & Change Management):* Architecture contracts, compliance reviews, and continuous governance.

### Weeks 45–48: Enterprise Financial Engineering & Cloud Governance
- **IT Portfolio Financial Modeling:** Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), Return on Investment (ROI), Net Present Value (NPV) of cloud modernization projects, software license management.
- **Enterprise Risk Management (ERM):** Aligning IT risk with COSO framework, cyber risk quantification (FAIR methodology), and data privacy governance (GDPR / CCPA / HIPAA).
- **Architecture Decision Frameworks:** Establishing an Architecture Review Board (ARB), authoring Enterprise Architecture Principles, and defining technical standards catalogs.
